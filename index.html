NW3 Baby Calendar

Free Google Calendar with baby & toddler classes around Hampstead, Swiss Cottage, and West Hampstead

📍 NW3, London · Free · Non-profit

How it works

  1. Pick your child's age and add the calendar to your phone in one tap.
  2. Every event has the time, place, price and the provider's website.
  3. It runs to the end of this term — leave your email and we'll send you the September one.

📅 Get your personalised calendar

📅 Add to Google Calendar
On iPhone or Mac? Add to Apple Calendar
Don't want it cluttering your calendar?
  1. It arrives as a separate calendar in your list — your own events aren't touched.
  2. Untick it to hide it, and tick it again when you're planning your week.
  3. Give it a soft colour so it doesn't shout over everything else.

✨ What's included

📬 Class timetables change when the September term starts. Add your email and we'll send you the updated calendar as soon as it's ready.

One email when the September calendar is ready — nothing else. Never shared, no marketing, free community project.

☕ Some places to go with a baby in NW3

📚 Swiss Cottage Library

88 Avenue Road, NW3 3HA · 📍 Map
A dedicated children's space with books and toys. Warm, quiet space.
FreeIndoorAccessible

🏛️ Keats Community Library

10 Keats Grove, NW3 2RR · 📍 Map
Community library in a lovely old building with a dedicated children's area. Next to the Heath. Very family-friendly, run by volunteers.
FreeIndoorAccessible

🧩 JW3

341-351 Finchley Road, NW3 6ET · 📍 Map
Big sandpit on the lower ground floor! Also a cafe, library, and children's events throughout the week. Indoor play area, warm and spacious.
SandpitCafeFreeIndoorAccessible

🎨 Camden Art Centre

Arkwright Road, NW3 6DG · 📍 Map
Beautiful green lawn where you can sit with a coffee while babies play. Art exhibitions, family workshops on weekends. Calm, creative atmosphere.
Green outdoor spaceCafeFreeIndoorAccessible

🌳 Parliament Hill Playground + Splash Pool

Parliament Hill, NW5 (near Lido) · 📍 Map
A paddling/splash pool for little ones in summer, playground, and wide open space to run around. Perfect picnic spot with views of the London skyline.
FreeOutdoorSplash pool

🧸 One O'Clock Club — Peggy Jay Centre

Nassington Road, NW3 2TX (Parliament Hill) · 📍 Map
Free indoor & outdoor play for under-5s. Baby area, small world play, construction, sand pit, climbing frame. Mon–Fri: morning session (10am–12pm, nominal charge) and afternoon session (1pm–3pm, voluntary charge).
AccessibleIndoor+Outdoor
