☕ Places to go with a baby in NW3

📚 Swiss Cottage Library

88 Avenue Road, NW3 3HA · 📍 Map
Free Rhyme Time (Tue/Fri 10:30), Stay & Play (Wed 10:30), dedicated children's library with books and toys. Warm, quiet space.
FreeIndoor

🏛️ Keats Community Library

10 Keats Grove, NW3 2RR · 📍 Map
Community library in a lovely old building. Rhyme Time Tue/Fri 11:00 (£5), dedicated children's area. Very family-friendly.
£5Community feel

🧩 JW3

341-351 Finchley Road, NW3 6ET · 📍 Map
Big sandpit on the lower ground floor! Also a cafe, library, and children's events throughout the week. Indoor play area, warm and spacious.
SandpitCafeIndoor

🎨 Camden Art Centre

Arkwright Road, NW3 6DG · 📍 Map
Beautiful green lawn where you can sit with a coffee while babies play. Art exhibitions, family workshops on weekends. Calm, creative atmosphere.
Green spaceFreeCafe

🌳 Parliament Hill Fields + Splash Pool

Parliament Hill, NW5 (near Lido) · 📍 Map
Huge green fields next to the Lido with a paddling/splash pool for little ones in summer, playground, and wide open space to run around. Perfect picnic spot with views of the London skyline.
FreeOutdoorSplash poolSummer

🧸 One O'Clock Club — Peggy Jay Centre

Nassington Road, NW3 2TX (Parliament Hill) · 📍 Map
Free indoor & outdoor play for under-5s. Baby area, small world play, construction, sand pit, climbing frame. Mon–Fri: morning session (10am–12pm, nominal charge) and afternoon session (1pm–3pm, voluntary charge). Right next to the Lido!
Free/VoluntaryUnder 5sIndoor+Outdoor
